1. Resolve the location and schedule first. The role requires San Francisco on-site, 5 to 6 days each week, and intense hours. State the real relocation timeline and assistance needs. The page currently makes no relocation promise.
2. Confirm current title, employment dates, and résumé figures. Add a redacted quarterly ranking or manager reference. If using the outbound qualification path, assemble delivered count, replies, positive replies, meetings, date range, and definitions. Distinguish booked from held meetings. No invented rates.
3. Record a 90-second walkthrough: 15 seconds on fit, 25 on one closed deal, 30 on the outbound engine, 20 on the first useful improvement at Varick. Use public or sanitized material.
4. Submit the official application with the tailored PDF, LinkedIn, and GitHub. The form has no dedicated portfolio field. After publication, add the portfolio URL to the résumé header so it travels with the application.
5. Send one concise message to the verified hiring owner through a confirmed public channel. Link the page and video. Ask for a working conversation. One useful follow-up after 4 to 5 business days is enough.
